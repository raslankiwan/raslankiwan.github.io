02 / How I build

AI-first,
engineer-owned.

I use Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor every day. The speed comes from the agents; the quality comes from how the work is set up and checked.

01 — ORCHESTRATE

The strongest model on the riskiest work

On Mercato, an orchestrating model owned the schema, Row-Level Security, and final verification, while parallel subagents scaffolded the apps and CI in separate directories. Model choice follows the cost of a mistake, not the size of the task.

02 — GUIDE

Rules the agents can read

Every project carries a CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md: the map, the commands, and the non-negotiables — tenant isolation, money as integers, safety rules that can never be weakened to make a change land.

03 — VERIFY

Nothing merges on trust

CI gates every change on types, lint, formatting, and tests, including isolation tests that fail if a new table isn't covered. Then I open the app and click the thing — several of the worst bugs were invisible to tests.
